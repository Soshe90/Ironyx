# Delete your Ironyx account and data

This page is for **Ironyx**, the offline-first workout tracker. Most of your
data never leaves your phone; an account exists only for the optional cloud
backup. This page explains how to delete what does leave it, and what stays.

## Delete your account from inside the app

1. Open Ironyx and go to **Settings → Account**.
2. Tap **Delete account**.
3. Type `DELETE` to confirm.

This permanently removes:

- your account (your email address and sign-in), and
- your cloud backup, if you made one.

It cannot be undone.

## Delete the workouts stored on your phone

Deleting your account does **not** erase the workouts, programs and body
metrics stored on your device. To remove them, open **Settings → Data
management → Delete everything** in the app, or uninstall Ironyx. If you never
created an account, this is the only step you need: Ironyx has no copy of your
data.

## If you cannot use the app
